# Changelog

## 2.2.0

- Added current-month collected total and confirmed-sale count.
- Added top-five best sellers by quantity.
- Excluded test, unpaid, submitted, and refunded transactions from analytics.
- Added optional local product-image support.
- Added a sanitized public template with configurable gym name, Venmo account,
  catalog, product images, and discount code.

## Earlier functionality

- Mobile checkout, product search, Venmo handoff, and floating pay button.
- Buyer-name capture and optional 30% coach discount.
- Staff transaction verification, editing, deletion, and refund tracking.
- Test mode, staff role, and payment-return confirmation flow.
